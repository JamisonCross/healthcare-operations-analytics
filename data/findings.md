# Synthetic appointment operations study

These results describe generated data, not a medical practice.

1. Behavioral health has the highest synthetic no-show rate: 24.25% across 664 appointments. Consider testing a supportive reminder workflow; this is not a causal result.

2. Provider C has the longest mean recorded wait: 21.84 minutes among 760 attended visits. Review slot length and workload before making staffing changes.

3. Appointments booked 21+ days ahead have an 18.73% no-show rate versus 10.09% for shorter booking windows. The relationship is deliberately encoded in this synthetic generator.
